Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Moonframe collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your photos and videos never leave your device.

Your photos and videos

Moonframe reads only the folders you choose. Thumbnails, face groups, duplicate fingerprints and place lookups are computed on your device and stored in the app's own container. Removing a folder or deleting the app removes them.

Vault

Vault items are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your vault password and, if you turn it on, by Touch ID or Face ID through Apple's Keychain. Moonframe never sees or sends your password, and it can't be recovered.

Network use

Moonframe connects to the internet only for Apple services:

  • Apple Maps, to draw map tiles and turn photo locations into place names in Places. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.
  • The App Store, to check and complete your Moonframe Pro purchase. Apple processes payment; Moonframe receives only whether the purchase is valid.

Children

Moonframe collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
