Support

Help with Moonframe for Mac and iPad

Open a request at github.com/dileepgaganr/dileepgaganr.github.io/issues. Include your device, its OS version and the Moonframe version from Settings. Please don't attach private photos.

Adding photos

Choose Add folder in the sidebar and pick any folder. Moonframe watches it for new files and never copies or moves anything on its own.

Opening photos from Finder

On Mac, choose Open With ▸ Moonframe in Finder. To always use it, pick Get Info ▸ Open with ▸ Moonframe ▸ Change All. If Moonframe can't read that folder yet, choose Browse this folder… to see the photos next to it.

Duplicates

Scan a folder to find exact and similar copies. Moonframe suggests which one to keep, and the others go to the Trash, so you can still get them back.

Vault

The vault encrypts photos and videos where they are. It locks when you leave it, switch apps, sleep, quit, or after 5 minutes idle. There is no password recovery. If you forget your password and can't use Touch ID or Face ID, the files can't be decrypted.

Moonframe Pro

Vault and Duplicates cleanup are open for your first month. After that, Moonframe Pro unlocks them with a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Already bought it on another device? Open Settings and choose Restore purchases. You can always view, unlock and restore files you already put in the vault.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
